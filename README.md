# 🌊 WakeScout

### Find better water before you burn the gas.

WakeScout is an Apple Shortcut built for wake surfers who know one universal truth:

**Flat water changes everything.**

Instead of staring at hourly weather forecasts and trying to mentally calculate which part of the day might produce the cleanest water, WakeScout does the comparison for you.

Choose a lake. Choose a day. WakeScout analyzes the forecast, evaluates the usable daylight windows, and identifies the best time to surf.

🌐 **[WakeScout.app](https://wakescout.app)**

---

## 🧠 How WakeScout Thinks

WakeScout does more than look for the hour with the lowest wind.

For the selected lake and date, WakeScout evaluates **every valid 3-hour daylight window**, moving forward in **1-hour increments**.

For example:

```text
6:00–9:00 AM
7:00–10:00 AM
8:00–11:00 AM
9:00 AM–12:00 PM
10:00 AM–1:00 PM
...
