# 🌊 WakeScout

### Find better water before you burn the gas.

WakeScout is an Apple Shortcut for wake surfers who know one universal truth:

**Flat water changes everything.**

Choose a Utah lake and a day. WakeScout analyzes the forecast, compares every usable 3-hour daylight window, and recommends the best time to surf.

🌐 **[WakeScout.app](https://wakescout.app)**

- An iPhone running **iOS 27 or newer**

## 🧠 How WakeScout Thinks

WakeScout evaluates every valid **3-hour daylight window** in **1-hour increments**.

For example:

```text
6:00–9:00 AM
7:00–10:00 AM
8:00–11:00 AM
9:00 AM–12:00 PM
...
```

Each window is ranked in this order:

1. Rain
2. Average wind
3. Maximum wind
4. Maximum gust

If two windows are effectively tied, the **earlier window wins**.

The goal is simple: turn a screen full of weather numbers into one useful recommendation.

---

## 🌬 Why Wind Matters

A forecast that says “5 mph wind” can hide a lot.

WakeScout looks at average wind, maximum wind, gusts, rain, and daylight because stable wind often matters more to water quality than a single hourly number.

A calm average with repeated hard gusts may produce worse water than slightly stronger but steadier wind.

WakeScout isn't trying to find perfect weather. It's trying to find the **best surfable water available that day**.

---

## 🌡 Water Temperature

WakeScout also provides estimated water temperatures for supported lakes.

These are **not live sensor readings**.

They use lake-specific monthly baselines built from historical observations, seasonal patterns, available lake data, and climatological estimates where direct measurements are limited.

Think:

> “About what should the water feel like this time of year?”

Not:

> “A sensor measured exactly 57.3°F ten minutes ago.”

Weather, depth, inflow, elevation, lake turnover, and location can all affect actual temperature.

WakeScout presents these values as **estimates**, not fake precision.

## 🔬 The WakeScout Philosophy

WakeScout doesn't pretend to replace a professional weather model.

It takes available weather data and makes it more useful for one very specific activity.

**Use understandable inputs.  
Compare them consistently.  
Avoid fake precision.  
Make the result useful on the dock.**

Forecasts can still miss local conditions caused by mountains, canyon winds, thunderstorms, shoreline geography, and boat traffic.

WakeScout helps stack the odds in your favor.

Mother Nature retains veto power.
