# 🌊 WakeScout

### Find better water before you burn the gas.

WakeScout is an Apple Shortcut built for wake surfers who know one universal truth:

**Flat water changes everything.**

Instead of staring at hourly weather forecasts and trying to mentally calculate which part of the day might produce the cleanest water, WakeScout does the comparison for you.

Choose a lake. Choose a day. WakeScout analyzes the forecast, evaluates the usable daylight windows, and identifies the best time to surf.

🌐 **[WakeScout.app](https://wakescout.app)**

---

## 🧠 How WakeScout Thinks

WakeScout does more than look for the hour with the lowest wind.

For the selected lake and date, WakeScout evaluates **every valid 3-hour daylight window**, moving forward in **1-hour increments**.

For example:

```text
6:00–9:00 AM
7:00–10:00 AM
8:00–11:00 AM
9:00 AM–12:00 PM
10:00 AM–1:00 PM
...
```

Each window is compared using the conditions that matter most to wake surfers.

WakeScout ranks them in this order:

1. Rain
2. Average wind
3. Maximum wind
4. Maximum gust

If two windows are effectively tied, the **earlier window wins**.

The result is a simple recommendation instead of a screen full of weather numbers.

---

## 🌬 Why Wind Gets So Much Attention

A forecast that says “5 mph wind” can hide a lot.

WakeScout looks beyond a single wind number.

A three-hour window with relatively calm average wind but repeated 15 mph gusts may produce much worse water than another window with slightly higher average wind but much more stable conditions.

That is why WakeScout considers:

- Average wind
- Maximum wind
- Maximum gust
- Rain
- Daylight

The goal is not to find perfect weather.

The goal is to identify the window most likely to produce the **best surfable water available that day**.

## 🌡 Water Temperature

WakeScout also provides an estimated water temperature for supported lakes.

These temperatures are **not live sensor readings**.

WakeScout uses lake-specific monthly temperature baselines built from historical observations, seasonal patterns, available lake data, and climatological estimates where direct measurements are limited.

Think of the number as:

> “About what should the water feel like this time of year?”

Not:

> “A sensor measured exactly 57.3°F ten minutes ago.”

Water temperatures can vary significantly because of weather, depth, inflow, elevation, lake turnover, and where you are on the lake.

WakeScout intentionally presents these values as **estimates**, not fake precision.

## 🏆 Lake Shootout

Sometimes the real question isn't:

> “When should I go to this lake?”

It's:

> **“Which lake should I go to?”**

Lake Shootout compares conditions across multiple lakes using the same WakeScout weather logic.

Instead of manually checking lake after lake, WakeScout can help identify which destination gives you the strongest shot at good water.

---

## 🔬 The WakeScout Philosophy

WakeScout is intentionally opinionated.

It isn't trying to replace a professional weather model or pretend it knows exactly what the lake will look like when you arrive.

It takes weather data that is already available and turns it into something more useful for a very specific activity.

The philosophy is simple:

**Use understandable inputs.  
Compare them consistently.  
Avoid fake precision.  
Make the result useful on the dock.**

A forecast is still a forecast.

Mountains, canyon winds, thunderstorms, shoreline geography, boat traffic, and local weather can all make reality different from the model.

WakeScout helps stack the odds in your favor.

Mother Nature retains veto power.
